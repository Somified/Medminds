# Medminds
An AI-powered patient case-taking platform that uses voice/touch interaction and OCR to capture medical history, digitize past records, and generate a structured, physician-ready summary—reducing consultation time and improving clinical documentation.

### AI-Powered Patient Case-Taking Software

> **Smart India Hackathon 2026 | PS ID: 26047**

MediKiosk is an AI-powered patient case-taking platform that helps patients record their medical history through **voice or touchscreen interaction** and digitize previous medical documents using **OCR**.

The collected information is converted into a **structured, physician-ready clinical summary**, helping reduce repetitive history-taking and consultation time.

---

## ✨ Features

* 🗣️ **AI Case Taking** — Voice and guided touchscreen interaction
* 🌐 **Multilingual Support** — Designed for diverse Indian patients
* 📄 **Medical Document OCR** — Extract information from prescriptions and reports
* 🧠 **Clinical Summary** — Convert responses into structured medical history
* 📋 **Medical Timeline** — Organize previous medical records chronologically
* 👨‍⚕️ **Doctor Dashboard** — Review and edit generated summaries
* ⚠️ **Red-Flag Detection** — Identify symptoms requiring priority attention

## 🎯 Objective

To reduce the burden of manual history-taking in high-volume OPDs by providing doctors with a **concise, structured patient history before the consultation begins**.

> **MediKiosk assists doctors. It does not replace clinical judgment or provide autonomous diagnosis.**
